Where this advice comes from
  • Weather: daily satellite records for your exact spot from NASA POWER (temperature, rain, sun, wind, humidity), updated every day and stored on this device — the advice works with no internet.
  • Map: world map outlines from Natural Earth (public domain), stored on this device so the location picker works offline.
  • Soil: the SoilGrids world soil map (acidity, sand, clay) for your spot.
  • Crop science: what each crop needs to grow, from the FAO EcoCrop database of 2,568 crops; how much water crops drink, from the FAO-56 handbook; how harvest follows from water used, from FAO's AquaCrop method.
  • Your choices: your priorities, your market knowledge, your water supply, and what grew here last.
  • The ranking: your priorities are turned into fair weights and every crop is scored and compared with a standard decision method (multi-criteria decision making). Two independent checks are run; when they disagree, we tell you it's a close call.

This is decision support, not a guarantee. Check with your local extension office before big changes. Expected harvest numbers are a comparison between crops, not a promise for your field.

Data: NASA POWER · ISRIC SoilGrids · FAO EcoCrop / FAO-56 / AquaCrop · Method: multi-criteria decision making (Gunawan et al. 2024)
